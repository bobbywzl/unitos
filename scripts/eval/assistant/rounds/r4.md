## What changed in round 4

The round measures the reader's report (the selection chat on a page with raw inline TeX: "fix the latex here" answered with a plan and changed nothing, and "do it" repeated the plan) and the fixes made for it, on a wider set of asks:

- **The act family** (10 cases): the selection chat (`/api/assistant/act`), its prompt built as the route builds it, its answer the route's JSON. A suggest action runs at once at score time, through the passes, and the judge reads what landed. Cases: a TeX line fixed, TeX written in words, the same fix on a shared document, a confirmation of suggestions already made, a question at the selection, a definition added, a line deleted, a line translated, a selection cut, a sentence made bold.
- **The TeX cases** (8 suggest cases on a new fixture, `linguistics-notes.md`, 23 rows): every inline TeX to Unicode, a process numbered, headings one level down, "ms" expanded, three questions merged into a paragraph, a summary at the top, questions as a checklist, core ideas in bold, a question that changes nothing.
- **JSON escapes** (`lib/tex-escapes.ts`, `derive/json.ts`, `assistant/plan.ts`, `docs/suggest-ops.ts`): a TeX command in a pass's JSON (`\text`, `\frac`, `\rightarrow`) read as an escape: `\t`, `\b`, `\f`, `\n`, `\r` became control characters without an error, and `\m` or `\p` broke the parse, so the ops' `find` words were never found and every op was skipped as not found. A failed parse is tried again with every unknown escape doubled; a parsed string gets its TeX commands back inside `$…$`; a `find` that fails is tried with its control characters restored; the pass prompts say to double every backslash. `scripts/qa/tex-escapes-check.ts` covers the escape cases.
- **The review box** (`components/docs/suggest/review.tsx`): the suggestions' summary, the current suggestion with Accept and Reject, Accept all and Reject all, and the changes that did not land with their reasons, open at once when a run lands, from the selection chat and the sidebar alike.
- **The act route**: a run that landed nothing says why; a run that landed says the suggestions are in the document; a confirmation of suggestions already made returns no action and points at the review box (act rule 2a).
- **The passes' answers** read an action's type under `type`, `kind`, or `action` and an op's name under `op`, `type`, `kind`, or `action`; the prompts name the key.
- **planOrder** (`lib/assistant/reorder.ts`): a plan of new blocks alone stands. A one pass that removed every block of its scope and wrote one new block in their place (three list lines merged into a paragraph) landed the removes and lost the new block; now the new block lands where the scope was (suggest-tex-merge-questions 5).
- **The loop**: rich-text fixtures are read as the page holds them, one row per paragraph, heading, and list line; a suggest action runs for real at score time; the act scoring shows the summary the route replies with (the first act judges saw an empty answer where the route shows the summary, and the seven cases were judged again); a Chinese description of one sentence passes the one-sentence check.

Round 4 changes no rule of the answer, edit, section, annotate, transcript, or confirm families: their scores are a second measurement of round 3's rules.

## What the answers show

Six checks fail in five suggest cases, every one on a run that the passes made: the suggest action's execution, new this round, shows faults the plan alone hid in round 3. Fault classes with two or more sightings, by family:

- **suggest, list lines**: a reorder of lines within a list and a move of two lines to another list land nothing, and the summary says the lines moved (suggest-notes-stage-order 5 → 2, suggest-notes-move-lines 4 → 2; round 3 judged their plans only). A list is one unit of the order plan (`richTextUnits`) and the page's move op moves top-level nodes, so no plan can move a line. Round 5's plan-module change.
- **suggest, list markers**: the one pass's rule 5 says a LIST block's text carries its marker, which is the article model; on a page a list line is its own row with no marker, so the pass wrote a literal "- " at the head of five lines (suggest-tex-unicode-all 2) and "1." to "4." into lines that keep their bullets (suggest-tex-number-process 4). The rule splits by the document's kind, and a list's kind changes with set_style.
- **suggest, instruction**: the instruction names a sentence, quotes the replacement words, or confines a document-wide fix to the paragraph where the assistant saw a fault (suggest-essay-fix 2 again: two dangling participles "fixed" on an essay the case holds has no error; suggest-draft-proofread 4: the three errors named with their paragraphs); a document-wide ask lists the blocks found instead of running whole (suggest-tex-unicode-all); a shorten restates the point in the assistant's words and the pass kept the paraphrase and cut "the shape of the argument" (suggest-essay-shorten 3). Fourth and fifth sightings since round 2.
- **act, the reply**: with a suggest action the route replies with the pass's summary, and the summary is two sentences with a trailing "No other word changes", never saying the change is pending in the document (act-tex-fix-line, act-add-definition, act-delete-line, act-translate-line, act-essay-cut-selection, act-essay-bold-sentence; reply 3 to 4 in each). The summary rule and the route's reply change in round 5.
- **act, questions**: a question at the selection runs four paragraphs with a paragraph on a point the reader did not ask (act-question-swinney 3); the sidebar's rule 6 has no counterpart in the act prompt. The sidebar shows the same on two question cases (suggest-tex-question-no-change answer 3, suggest-essay-question answer 3).
- **act, shared document**: the reply says the document cannot change, then argues the LaTeX "needs no fix" in place of the readable line the rule asks for (act-tex-shared-fix 2). One sighting; scope-shared-import (4) gives the words.
- **section**: a note that summarizes a named part reads one paragraph of it and skips the method's cost (section-paper-method-note 3 in rounds 3 and 4); the lecturer is "He" (section-lecture-summary-doc, as in round 3). The add_note shape holds: the family rises 3.91 → 4.43 (docs-limit-notes 3 → 4, notes-questions 3 → 4, podcast-document 4 → 5, meeting-action-items 4 → 5).
- **answer**: "complete" stays the worst criterion (answer-docs-limits-count 4 → 3 for the limits it leaves out; answer-lecture-revenue 5 → 4 for the arithmetic behind a figure); no answer rule changed, so the drop of 0.14 is the judges' reading of the same faults.
- **edit**: every case 4 or 5. edit-essay-formal 3 → 4 with the change window naming its spans; the answer still runs 50 words in one sentence (edit-essay-formal, edit-essay-fix-mistakes, edit-essay-runon) and never says what new words say (edit-essay-conclusion, as edit-paper-insert-after in round 3).
- **annotate**: one case under 4 (annotate-law-question-each-rule 3: a sixth comment on a paragraph that is no rule's first); the rest 4 or 5.
- **transcript** 5.00 and **confirm** 4.75 as in round 3.

The TeX report itself: act-tex-fix-line lands both ops (Bank₁ and Bank₂) at once with no second turn (at_once 5, on_selection 5, exact 5), and every TeX suggest case but the two above scores 4 or 5; the escape repair holds on every pass of the round.

## Decision

Kept, all of it: the act family and the TeX cases (new measurement), the JSON escape repair and the review box (the report's case lands, and nothing is skipped as not found in the round), the act route's turn text, the key tolerance, planOrder's plan of new blocks alone (suggest-tex-merge-questions 5), and the loop's real suggest runs. The mean falls 4.38 → 4.27 because the round measures more: 31 new cases, among them a family at 3.70, and the suggest action's real run, which turns two list-line moves from 5 and 4 to 2 and 2. On the 74 shared cases, 7 rose, 8 fell, 59 stayed, and no family whose rules changed fell.

Round 5, one change per fault class: the page moves a list line (the order plan's units at the list item, the page's move op on a list item), the one pass's list rule for a page and its one-sentence summary, the suggest instruction rule (a document-wide change runs whole; the instruction names the kind of change, never the errors found or the replacement words), the act reply says the suggestions are in the document and a question at the selection stops as rule 6 does, a part's summary reads every block under its heading, and the lecturer is never he or she. New cases for the ops no case exercises.
