## What changed in round 10 (commit 9684304)

- **One chip for blocks named together**: block tags side by side render as one chip with their count (¶3) that scrolls to the first block and flashes each (`lib/block-tags.ts`). Every changed, marked, or quoted block takes its own tag; a whole-document change takes none.
- **Lookups**: a question of who, which, when, where, how many, or whether that the material answers in its own words ends at the passage that answers it, its reason in a clause, with no limit, warning, or purpose line. A number keeps an exception the material states; a condition keeps the material's own word. A count the document never states says "by my count"; look-alike figures left out are named in one sentence.
- **The nearest change, at once**: a change the document does not take (a new line in a recording, a block in slides or sheets) is said in one clause, and the same answer proposes the nearest change it takes, with its actions (`NEAREST_CHANGE_RULE`).
- **Change answers**: claim no more than the plan; a narrowing says "only" before what it keeps; a heading's words go in quotes.
- **Documents and notes**: a document for a named reader holds only what that reader says or does; a summary takes one or two quote lines a point and stays under half its source; key numbers are the few the point rests on; a note on numbered points gives each its own line; a "not answered" note quotes the closest line for each thing asked.
- **Math in words**: a neutral example in every prompt; a name keeps its Unicode subscript.
- **Edit descriptions**: words only added or removed read with the word before them; up to four changes in full; more as the three largest and a count; a rewrite that grows names its length.
- **Judge view**: tags side by side read as one chip; a block's change of kind is listed.
- **Eleven new cases** on bond prices, Raft, photosynthesis in Chinese, and the lecture (commit 0dddc07).

## Decision

Kept, but for one clause. The mean holds at 4.52 and rises to 4.55 on the shared cases; annotate (+0.37), answer (+0.14), confirm (+0.25), and transcript (+0.10) rise on the round's targets, and suggest, section, and scope hold. Act falls 4.83 → 4.58: of its three falls, one comes from a round 10 change, the clause that dropped a formula restating its sentence, which is reverted (the name keeps its Unicode subscript); the other two are wording nits no round 10 change touched. Edit falls 4.61 → 4.57 on two nits.

The loop stops here at the owner's request. Round 11 candidates, not made:
1. The number check lets a figure stand when the same sentence derives it by one operation from figures the document prints, and the prompt says a worked example uses the document's own results.
2. A question whether a claim holds or will last is not a lookup; a lookup gives every figure the material attaches to each thing it names; "only" never stands where the passage holds more.
3. Key numbers are the ones the document's own summary lines give.
4. A block is cited once per answer.
5. A place the reader named by what it says is named back by its own words.
6. Note descriptions stay under 25 words and never list the quoted spans.
