## What changed in round 11

- **The document reading** (`lib/assistant/document-reading.ts`, SPEC.md §7): at This page scope a document past the whole threshold (120,000 characters) is no longer sent whole and cut at 480,000; the message reads the document's opening, the parts it names whole, a sketch of every part for an overview message, and the blocks ranked highest against its words with their neighbours and headings, to 200,000 characters. The skeleton's reading passes pick when the skeleton is built; the lexical pass (BM25) until then. The system prefix puts the gist, the contents, the layers, and the notes first and the passages last, so the prefix caches: it hashes the same across the round's questions.
- **The loop reads long fixtures the same way** (`digest.ts` fixtureReading, `run.ts`): a fixture past the threshold renders the picked prefix and the prompt's picked read mode.
- **book-probability**: the first two chapters of Grinstead and Snell's Introduction to Probability as the parser read them, 648 blocks, 161k characters. Six answer cases: a lookup, a follow-up by pronoun, a count over a named section, an explanation from chapter 2, a summary of both chapters, a question the material does not cover.
