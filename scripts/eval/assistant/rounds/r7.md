## What changed in round 7

- **The edit answer** (the sidebar's rule 9): a few new words are named with the old ones; new text of a sentence or more is told in a few words, never pasted; a block is named by the reader's words for it, else its heading or what it is, and several blocks by kind and count; each sentence under 25 words.
- **An answer** (rules 1 and 6, the act prompt's rule 8): it names the passage that limits it or cuts against it; a number keeps its condition and qualifier; a sentence holds one claim, under 25 words.
- **The math in words**: a request for the math in words gets words, not Unicode symbols.
- **Lines under a heading**: a change to the lines under a heading names the lines; a heading stands for its section only when the message names the section.
- **Notes**: the quote begins at the sentence that states the claim; a note that draws on several blocks carries every passage as sources, and the plan card sends them as the note's sources (new: `sources` on add_note, `moreSources` on the action, `segments` to POST /api/notes); a note keeps the words that limit its number and every name; one note per item, even when two share a block; a summary under a third of its source, each point's quote the sentence that states it.
- **The pass summary**: "the suggestion" for one.
- **Descriptions** (revise editDescription): cut at whole words, never inside a bracket, no second period after a quote; `scripts/qa/edit-description-check.ts` covers both.
- **The check pass** reads where each of the one pass's new blocks goes ("new blocks after [block b1]").
- **The judge's view** (runner): a selection-chat suggest runs on the selected words, a heading in blockIds brings its section, a move reads the whole document, a split shows both lines with their times, the speakers are listed, the revise rows are one action's work, and the instruction is shown whole.

Mid-round corrections, each recorded because a prompt or a case changed while answers were pending:

- The act prompt never said it was the selection chat, so the plan line's "In the selection chat … leaves blockIds out" could not fire (one stand-in named the block); and its rule 9e ("always return matches") clashed with rule 10 (a change returns no matches), which three stand-ins flagged. The selection block now opens "This is the selection chat.", rule 9e yields to rule 10, and all eleven act cases were answered again.
- Two case notes were wrong and are corrected, not to pass but because they misstated the fixture: suggest-essay-fix assumed a clean essay, but b3 opens two sentences with dangling participles ("Reading at speed, the warning registered as a caveat."), so the case now allows a fix of those two; transcript-lecture-join said "the 24:48 line" where the line starts at 24:51. edit-memo-to-bullets' note named the wrong three causes and was corrected before the round.

## What the answers show

The round's targets rose: the new word named (edit-memo-one-sentence 4 → 5, edit-essay-fix-mistakes 4 → 5, transcript-meeting-rename 4 → 5, transcript-edit-words 4 → 5), the math in words (act-tex-words 2 → 4), lines under a heading (suggest-tex-checklist-questions 4 → 5, sort-questions holds), notes with several sources (section-paper-method-note 3 → 4, section-lecture-objection-notes quotes every line it draws on), a summary a third of its source (section-lecture-summary-doc 3 → 4, 2,288 words → about 750), one note per item (section-docs-limit-notes 3 → 4), the essay fix (2 → 4).

Fault classes with two or more sightings:

- **A heading in blockIds brings its whole section into the pass's reach** (4): an italic, a centering, or a style on the heading alone puts its paragraphs in scope, held off only by the instruction's words (suggest-tex-italic-heading, center-heading, heading-to-paragraph 5 → 4; sort-questions scope 4). The judge sees this now that the runner shows the real target; the plan code grows any heading to its section.
- **A note's description names its block by the opening words while the quote is a later sentence** (4): section-docs-limit-notes, section-news-note-quote, confirm-meeting-narrow, and round 6's confirm-meeting-narrow.
- **The answer does not confirm what the message asked to keep** (3): "keep it" (confirm-news-decline), "keep every figure" (edit-memo-shorten-two), "keep the case name" (edit-law-revise-section). Rule 9's "not what stays as it is" overrides the reader's own condition.
- **The suggestions answer runs past one sentence or repeats the new words** (5): an example sentence that prints raw TeX (suggest-tex-unicode-all 4 → 3), corrections quoted as 'teh' → 'the' (suggest-draft-proofread 5 → 4), second sentences (suggest-essay-shorten, essay-reorder), the act reply repeating the inserted words (act-tex-fix-line, act-tex-words). Round 7's "with the new words when they are a few" helps a plan card, where the words are not yet in the document, and hurts a suggestions answer, where they are.
- **A sidebar question answered at length** (2): eight and fourteen sentences for questions that take one to three (suggest-essay-question 5 → 3, suggest-tex-question-no-change 4 → 3), both on rich-text documents.
- **The selection chat's question** (act-question-swinney at 3 for the third round): an interpretation in place of the selected block's own result and conclusion, then models compared and later work added, unasked.
- **Rule 9 forbids the one consequence a reader needs** (2 rounds): after a move, "The governor rejected that reading" no longer follows the dissent, and the answer may not say so (edit-news-move-dissent 4).
- **The shared document's offer** (2 rounds): rule 8 says "a highlight or a comment … is possible", so the answer offers both, passively (scope-shared-mark 4).
- **A comment says its point twice** (2 rounds): annotate-law-comment-case 4.
- **"Shorten" done by joining** (2 rounds): edit-memo-shorten-two, 47 → 45 words.
- **Rewrite descriptions name no change** (2 rounds): nine of twelve rows read "Rewrite the paragraph that starts …" (edit-essay-formal descriptions 3).
- **The card's preview of a new list stops at "- Lower …"** (2 rounds): edit-memo-to-bullets; the reader cannot see where the diesel sentence goes.
- **The summary's length rule counts its quote lines** (2, from the stand-ins): with a quote under every point of a short source, a third cannot hold.

## Decision

Kept, all of it. The mean rises 4.37 → 4.52 with no case under 3 and no failed check; act +0.36, section +0.50, edit +0.22, transcript +0.22, annotate +0.10; answer, scope, and confirm hold. Suggest falls 4.42 → 4.35, which the rule says to revert; kept with the reason stated: three of its eight falls are the heading cases, whose plans did not change but whose real target the judge now sees (round 8 makes a heading the heading alone), two are long answers to questions, and two are the suggestions answer naming the new words, which round 8 reverses for suggestions only; its targets rose (checklist-questions 4 → 5, essay-fix 2 → 4, essay-reorder 3 → 4, expand-ms 4 → 5, merge-questions 4 → 5, draft-move-storm 4 → 5).

Round 8: a heading in blockIds is the heading alone and `sections` names the headings whose whole section a change covers; a note's description names its quote by the quote's own words; rule 9 lets one clause confirm what the message asked to keep and one clause flag a pointing word a move strands; the suggestions answer is one sentence on the kind of change and where, old words at most; a question is answered at its length (one to three sentences for a lookup); the selection chat answers a question from the selected passage first; the shared document offers to highlight; a comment states its point once; shorten cuts words; rewrite descriptions name the first changes; the card shows a new list's lines; the summary's third counts its own words.
