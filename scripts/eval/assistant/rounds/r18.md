## What changed in round 18

- **A heading's label words name nothing.** After the stemmer, "chapters" in the message matched the one word of every "Chapter N" heading, so a message over chapters 1 and 2 named every chapter. The label words (chapter, section, part, appendix, unit, lecture, week) are left out of the word rules; the number names the part. The chapters regex collects every number of "chapters 1 and 2".
- **A message over the parts it names reads a sketch of them**, every heading and the first block under it, where a message that names a part for another purpose reads it whole. "Summarize chapters 1 and 2" reads 391 blocks (147k characters) of the two chapters; "each exercise of section 1.2" reads section 1.2 whole.
- **A bare chapter label takes its title's depth.** The fixture sets "Chapter 1" as a smaller heading than its title "Discrete Probability Distributions" on the next line, so the chapter's part ended one block in, and the overview case read 87 blocks. The label and the title heading right after it are one heading at the title's depth; the chapter runs to the next chapter.
- **A numbered part bounds "each"** in the depth module: "each exercise of section 1.2" was "across the document" (the bounded rule wanted "this section"), which made the reading sketch section 1.2 instead of reading it whole; the first run of the notes case wrote two notes of four and said the rest was not read. "section 1.2", "chapter 3", "第三章" bound the message to a part.
