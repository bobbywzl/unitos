## What changed in round 2

Six changes, each for a fault class round 1 showed in two or more cases:

- **Rule 9, refined** (`lib/prompts/synthesis.ts`): the answer beside a change is a statement of what the plan does, never an offer ("I can…") and never the message said back; the old words and the new when a few words change.
- **Answer rule 6**: stop at the answer; no aside on a passage that neither answers nor contradicts, no derived figure, no advice; at most one sentence for the reader's purpose; a lookup ends with its one to three sentences.
- **create_document** (`lib/assistant/plan.ts`, `lib/assistant/document.ts`, `POST /api/documents/generated`): a message that asks for a new document is one action whose markdown becomes the document's blocks, every `>` quote line a block that links back to the passage it copied; the plan card shows the title and the count of quotes; Undo deletes the document. Rule 10 (synthesis) and rule 12 (act) say when to use it.
- **Change window** (`lib/assistant/diff-window.ts`): the plan card's preview of an edit shows the changed words with the words around them, not the whole block twice.
- **editDescription** (`lib/assistant/revise.ts`): an edit a pass made describes its own change ("Change “a little context” to “some context”.") in place of the pass's one description on every edit.
- **Auto Thinking** (`lib/assistant/depth.ts`, `thinking.ts`): the default thinking choice reads the message and runs a lookup, a one-place change, or a confirmation at low effort, a question of meaning at the deep effort, a change across the document or several asks at the highest. Verified by `scripts/qa/depth-check.ts` (26 messages), not by this benchmark: the stand-in agents run at their own effort.

## What the answers show

Every mechanical check passes except two: suggest-draft-proofread narrowed a whole-essay proofread to the three blocks where it found errors (blockIds set, "Fix three errors"), and edit-essay-formal's one pass rewrote words beyond the first person and the contractions the message named ("throw at" → "inflict on", the opening sentence recast), which the judge capped at 2.

Fault classes with two or more sightings, by family:

- **transcript**: the answer names a line by the tag's raw seconds ("65.0s–102.0s") where the reader sees 1:05 (transcript-edit-words, transcript-remove-line, transcript-split-line). The plans themselves are exact; the lecture and meeting cases score 5.
- **answer**: the answer carries more than the question needs: a second paragraph on a passage the question did not ask about (answer-news-followup), two context sentences (answer-essay-connection), three paragraphs for a one-line question (answer-zh-regulation), a point restated three times (answer-law-apply), a closing paragraph for the reader's purpose (suggest-essay-question), "I read all twelve blocks" (scope-project-change). Rule 6 as written did not stop it.
- **scope**: a bare refusal on the shared document, with no words and no offer (scope-shared-import, rounds 1 and 2).
- **section**: one note per X reads only the blocks under the heading X (section-memo-risk-notes, rounds 1 and 2: the fuel reversal in b7 is never a note); a note's body restates a neighbouring sentence while its quote is another (section-podcast-topic-notes, three of seven notes), or is a label plus the quote (section-news-note-quote).
- **edit**: the answer names a block twice, by its chip and its opening words or its ordinal (edit-essay-move-storm, edit-essay-costs-list, edit-memo-remove-block, edit-history-move); a description says "the paragraph" without naming it (edit-essay-costs-list, edit-essay-runon); a revise rewrites words the command did not reach (edit-essay-formal, edit-law-revise-section).

One sighting each, held for a second: the proofread narrowed to the errors found (suggest-draft-proofread); a study guide that restates nearly every line of the notes (section-notes-study-guide); a note that needs one source per block it takes a number from (section-paper-method-note); a moved line sent to the end of its list (suggest-notes-move-lines).

## Decision

Kept: rule 9 (the plans of every change family stay exact; annotate +0.43, section +0.40, confirm +0.75, transcript +0.11; the edit and suggest drops are a pass's over-rewrite, a narrowed proofread, and two judge nits on exact plans, none in the sentence rule 9 governs), create_document (section +0.40; the podcast document 2 → 5, the study guide 2 → 4, the meeting's action items 4 → 5), the change window and editDescription (no judge faulted a plan card), Auto Thinking (not measured here; the depth check passes).

Reverted in effect: answer rule 6 did not raise its family (4.21 → 4.14; answer-paper-absent 5 → 4 for a figure it stopped before) and on its own would go. Round 3 puts a sharper wording in its place: no context the question did not ask for, no point made twice, no account of how the answer was found, a lookup in one paragraph. If round 3's answer mean is not above round 1's 4.21, rule 6 goes.

Fixed in the loop this round: the first create_document cases needed the runner's simulate to carry documents and the judge prompt to show them; two section cases took `sections` in place of a note count the plan could not know.

The first create_document cases (section-podcast-document 5, section-meeting-action-items 5, section-lecture-summary-doc, section-notes-study-guide 4) show the action works: every quote resolved to its block, the document's shape followed the ask.
