## What changed in round 19

Rule 3a of the synthesis prompt: a tag goes only on a block whose own text states the claim; a heading block states its title and nothing else; a step the material does not state takes no tag and stands in the same sentence as the claim it follows from. Round 18's judges found tags on heading blocks and on blocks that do not say what the sentence says in three of seven book cases.

## What the answers show

- Up: answer-zh-regulation 4 to 5, book-exercises-dice-count 4 to 5 (the count and the list agree in the opening sentence; 17(c) named after), book-overview 3 (r12) to 4.
- Down: answer-docs-limits-count 4 to 3 (the refill rates left out; four tags side by side on one sentence), answer-lecture-revenue 5 to 4 (three tags on one figure where one block shows the working), answer-meeting-actions 5 to 4 (a figure tagged to two blocks that do not hold it), book-not-covered 5 to 4 (says the rest was not read where the reading held every mention; a closing sentence repeats the opening).
- The evidence fault the rule aimed at still stands in seven cases: a reason of the assistant's own under a block's tag (meeting-decision, podcast-where, raft, zh-photosynthesis, problem-of-points), a figure tagged to a block without it (meeting-actions, overview). The rule moved none of them.

## Decision

Not kept: the family mean did not rise, and the fault class the rule targets appears as often as before. Reverted. The next lever is code, not prompt: after the answer, check each tagged sentence's figures against the tagged blocks' text, and when a figure stands in no tagged block but in one block of the reading, add that block's tag.
