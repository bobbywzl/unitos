## Decision

Kept, all of it. The mean rises 4.46 → 4.52, and 4.46 → 4.57 on the shared cases; six families rise or hold. Annotate falls 4.60 → 4.27 on three one-point drops (one chip for two paragraphs, a 27-word answer sentence, a comment's jargon) and its new case at 4 (a marked block without a chip); transcript falls 4.89 → 4.80 on its new case alone, while its nine shared cases rise to 5.00. Answer holds (4.14 → 4.13) with the length fault moving from closing sentences to whole extra paragraphs on who/which questions. Each fall traces to a round 9 clause that round 10 corrects in place (the one-tag clause, the count clause) or to a fault no round 9 change touched.

Round 10 (commit 9684304): block tags side by side render as one chip with their count, which scrolls to the first and flashes each, so every changed, marked, or quoted block takes its own tag, and a whole-document change takes none; a question of who, which, when, where, how many, or whether that the material answers in its own words is a lookup, with its reason in a clause and no limit, warning, or purpose line; a number keeps an exception the material states, and a condition keeps the material's own word; "by my count" in two words, and look-alike figures left out named in one sentence; a change the document does not take is said in one clause and the nearest change it takes is proposed in the same answer with its actions; a change answer claims no more than the plan, a narrowing says "only" before what it keeps, a heading's words go in quotes; a document for a named audience holds only what that audience says or does; a summary takes one or two quote lines a point and stays under half its source with its quotes; key numbers are the few the point rests on; a note on numbered points gives each its own line; the math-in-words example is neutral in every prompt, a name keeps its Unicode subscript, and a formula that only restates its sentence is dropped; edit descriptions read words only added with the word before them, list up to four changes in full, name the three largest of more, and give a rewrite's length; the judge sees one chip for tags side by side and each block whose kind changes. Eleven new cases on four documents the assistant loop had not used (bond prices, Raft, photosynthesis in Chinese, and the lecture): a which-question, a table row, a glossary, times in ms, a why-question, a section in a neutral voice, the selection on a number, a narrowed confirmation, a line a recording does not take, a lookup in Chinese, and a rewrite with a worked example.
