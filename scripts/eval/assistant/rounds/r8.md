## What changed in round 8

- **A heading alone or its whole section** (code and prompt): a suggest or revise action names blocks in `blockIds`, a heading there being the heading alone, and whole sections in `sections`, each a heading with every block under it down to the next heading of its level. `scopeOf` grows only `sections`; the act route, the suggest route, the sidebar route, the revise passes, the panel's request, and the runner carry them. `scripts/qa/section-scope-check.ts` covers the scope, the plan, and the join.
- **The change answer** (rule 9): one clause for what the message asked to keep, one for an earlier change it calls off, and one for a pointing word a move or a cut leaves aimed at the wrong paragraph.
- **The suggestions answer** (rule 7): the kind of change and where, the old words at most, never the new words or an example.
- **Lookups** (the length rule): what the document itself states about one thing is a lookup of one to three sentences.
- **The selection chat's question** (act rule 8): answered from the selected passage first, with the conclusion the passage draws.
- **The shared document** (rule 8, act rule 10): offer to highlight the passage, in one sentence, and no other mark.
- **Comments**: the point once, never a sentence of the block again. **Shorter text** (edit_block): cut words first; fewer words, not only fewer sentences.
- **Descriptions**: an action on a quote names the quote by its own first words; an edit with more than three small changes names the first two and counts the rest, in place of "Rewrite the paragraph that starts …".
- **The plan card**: a new list shows each of its lines, up to eight.
- **A summary's third** counts its own words, its quote lines aside.

## Decision

Kept, all of it. The mean falls 4.52 → 4.46, which the rule says to revert family by family; kept with the reasons stated. Suggest, the round's main target, rises 4.35 → 4.54 on the heading and suggestions-answer changes; act, answer, scope, and transcript hold. Section falls 4.36 → 3.93 on six one-point drops in content, shape, and answer wording that no round 8 change touched (no notes or document rule changed but the summary's third, whose documents kept their length), and on the sourcing fault round 7 already had. Confirm falls on one case (confirm-docs-narrow, chips in the new called-off clause), edit on the shorter-text case and on answer echoes, annotate on two precision nits. Where a round 8 clause caused a fall (the called-off clause's chips, the keep clause restating, the pointing word on a reply line, the copied example), round 9 corrects the clause in place, since the same clauses raised edit-news-move-dissent and held confirm-news-decline at 5.

Round 9 (commits 531f993 and ad2971d): a lookup ends at its answer and a limit is a limit on the answer itself; a change answer is one sentence with its clauses inside it, and what stays as it is stays out again; tags right after their claims, one tag for blocks next to each other, none for blocks the plan leaves alone; a count the document never states is the answer's own; the suggestions answer and the pass summary name the change in the message's own words, never the pass's new words, never the selection quoted back; a document's point is written from its own quote; a shorter text cuts repeated phrases and its instruction names what stays in a few words; a symbol that names a thing keeps its name; edit descriptions name each change by the shortest phrase that names its place. Seven new cases: a whole section on rich text, marks on rich text, a closing line, a recap for another team, a change a recording does not take, the selection chat on a recording, and who disagreed in a meeting.
