## What changed (`lib/derive/visualize.ts`, `lib/derive/simulate.ts`)

- **Diagrams fit the card.** The layout tries the model's direction at the widest text, then narrower text, then the other direction, and keeps the first no wider than 460 units; details and edge labels at 13, not 12. Minimum text in the card went from 4.7–9 px to 6–10.4 px; 7 of 12 diagrams now have no text under 9 px.
- **Labels never cover arrows.** The gap between ranks holds the widest label crossing it; two labels on one spot slide apart along their curves.
- **Loops and back edges.** An edge from a node to itself draws as a loop; a back edge's arc clears every box it passes.
- **Words stay whole.** A Latin word is never cut mid-word; CJK still wraps by characters.
- **Simulations read.** The law fits the frame (one line down to 14, else two lines). Axes stop at round values in plain digits (0 and 10,000, not −800 and 1.1e4). The clock shows a unit when the spec gives one; `levels` draw the passage's thresholds as labeled dashed lines; ODE variables take legend labels.
- **The Schrödinger solver is right.** The grid follows the physics (phase step, barrier width, packet width; 128–2,048 points), drawn from a smoothed 200-point copy. A 0.25 nm barrier now transmits 24.8% (the article: 24%); the old grid gave 10%.

## What is left
