# Round 0: the baseline

Production as it stands: the draw prompt, the check pass, and the server's renderers, on 66 cases over 19 documents (12 new ones: physics, epidemiology, physiology, distributed systems, algorithms, economics, law, engineering, finance, history, and a Chinese biology text). An Opus 5.5 subagent answered every prompt as the model; a separate subagent judged each picture blind — first what it says at first sight, then against the passage.

| kind | cases | overall |
|---|---|---|
| decline | 9 | 4.11 |
| animation | 3 | 4.00 |
| picture (the model's own SVG) | 33 | 3.88 |
| diagram (laid out by the server) | 8 | 2.62 |
| simulation (integrated by the server) | 7 | 2.43 |

Overall 3.58 of 5. The decision to draw or decline agreed with the case's expectation in 65 of 66. The weakest criterion was legibility in 21 cases, glance in 12.

## What the pictures show

- **The server's drawing is the bottleneck, not the model.** The model's own SVG pictures were nearly lint-clean; every diagram and simulation the server drew had a fault. Diagrams laid out left to right came out 600–820 units wide, so text drew at 4.7–7 px in the 320 px card. Simulation titles ran off both edges; axes read "1.1e4" and "−800 people"; the clock read "3.6e3".
- **The Schrödinger simulation was wrong.** 128 grid points put 1.6 points across a 0.25 nm barrier: the packet transmitted 10% where the equation gives 24%. A judge caught it independently from the SVG's path data.
- **Diagram layout faults**: an edge label wider than the gap between ranks covered its own arrowhead; a node's edge to itself was dropped silently (the model avoided the diagram kind for a state machine because of it); back edges cut through wide boxes.
- **Declines were right but long**: 65–120 words, some in the first person, one naming an internal block id.
- **Pictures**: labels running past their space ("Sparse routinc"), prose competing with the marks, labels floating away from what they name, a curve drawn between two stated points as if its shape were known, text at 14 units = 9 px in the card.
- **The check pass** kept 43, replaced 8. Its replacements fixed real faults (a caption that misstated a cause, an unreadable diagram redrawn as a picture), but one replacement drew bars off scale (heat-timescale, accuracy 2): the check's own picture is never checked.

## Efficiency

Output tokens per case (visible output, reasoning excluded): decline ≈ 260, simulation ≈ 640, diagram ≈ 850, picture ≈ 1,500, animation ≈ 3,800. Two calls per drawn picture. The check's SVG-source input is the largest part of its prompt; server-drawn kinds put 5–75k characters of SVG in front of a model that cannot change it.

## Decision

Round 1 fixes the server's drawing (no model change). Round 2 tests a draw prompt with a rule for each fault class above.
