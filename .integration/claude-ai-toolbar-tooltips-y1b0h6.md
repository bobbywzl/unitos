# claude/ai-toolbar-tooltips-y1b0h6

**Intent:** Every control in the AI toolbar (selection popover and article menu), the sidebar (rail and tray panels), and the top bar gets a short caption of what it is and does, opening beside the control on mouse hover — one shared tooltip layer instead of native `title` attributes.

**Files:**
- `src/components/tooltip.tsx` — new. `TooltipLayer`: one client component that renders the caption for whatever control carries `data-tooltip`. Mouse hover shows it after 350 ms; moving straight from one control to the next shows the next at once (250 ms warm window); keyboard focus (`:focus-visible`) shows it too. Hides on a press, Escape, a scroll, leaving the control, window blur, and when the control leaves the page or moves (a 150 ms watch). `data-tooltip-side` picks top/bottom/left/right (bottom default); the caption flips when that side has no room and stays 8 px inside the viewport. A control wider than 240 px or taller than 80 px (the tray's resize bar, a document title) anchors the caption at the pointer, not its center. Touch never shows one. The control gets `aria-describedby` while its caption is up. Styling: `bg-ink text-paper`, 12 px, max 260 px wide, no arrow — inverts with the theme on its own.
- `src/app/layout.tsx` — mounts `<TooltipLayer />` once, beside the feedback button, so `data-tooltip` works on every page.
- `src/app/globals.css` — `tooltip-in` keyframes (120 ms fade, reduced-motion guarded).
- `src/components/reader/workspace.tsx` — header: back link, pending count chip, guide, tray resize bar, Notes full page link. Rail: collapse/expand, Assistant, Notes, Distill, Graph, Annotations, Edits, More — all `data-tooltip-side="left"` (`RAIL_TOOLTIP_SIDE`), opening over the tray, away from the right edge.
- `src/components/reader/document-bar.tsx` — documents pill (side right, so the caption never covers the list that opens below on hover), + add, per-document ⋯ and its menu items (re-parse, parse as article, open as handwritten, recommend links, print, detach; side right). Document rows keep the native `title={d.title}`: that is the row's full title when truncated, data rather than a description.
- `src/components/context-tab.tsx`, `src/components/collab/share-control.tsx`, `src/components/collab/history-control.tsx`, `src/components/notebook-title.tsx`, `src/components/collab/person-badge.tsx` — header controls: `title` → `data-tooltip`.
- `src/components/reader/reader-interactions.tsx` — article menu pill and its items (side right, clear of the menu), search, font select, Done, Distill button; the selection popover rows (Assistant, mic, Run/Stop, Extract, Explain, Simplify, Comment, Link, Close link, Add to notes, voice) with `toolSide` = the toolbox's outer side (away from the text); the highlight dots with `hueSide` (above the bubble, or below when it has dropped under the toolbox); the annotation card's recolor dots; the extract card; the side cards' drag handles, delete buttons, simplified sentences, chat Send/Stop; the pending-link banner ✕.
- `src/components/reader/reader.tsx` — edit toolbar: `title` → `data-tooltip`, plus `aria-label` on the glyph-only buttons (¶, H1–H3, •, 1., B, I, U, ⇤, ⇥), since the title used to be their only name; Remove ¶ gains a caption. `src/components/reader/document-title.tsx`, `src/components/reader/reader-panes.tsx` (reader view button, side right; second pane select) likewise.
- `src/components/assistant/assistant-panel.tsx` — scope buttons, Ask/Stop, and the three tasks (new `TASK_TITLE` captions). `src/components/panels/distill-panel.tsx`, `src/components/panels/annotations-panel.tsx`, `src/components/outline/selection-bar.tsx` — `title` → `data-tooltip`.
- `src/lib/i18n/dict/panes.ts`, `reader.ts`, `assistant.ts` — new caption keys, en + zh (`allCorporaTitle`, `pendingCountTitle`, `assistantTitle`, `notesTitle`, `distillTitle`, `annotationsTitle`, `editHistoryTitle`, `moreTitle`, `readerViewTitle`, `secondPaneDocumentTitle`, `removeParagraphTitle`, `documentListTitle`, `documentActionsTitle`, `addDocumentTitle`; `assistantMenuTitle`, `frequentAskTitle`, `askAssistantTitle`, `readerFontTitle`, `assistantToolTitle`, `runTitle`, `sendTitle`, `explainTitle`, `simplifyTitle`, `commentTitle`, `closeLinkTitle`; `askTitle`, `taskContradictionsTitle`, `taskGapsTitle`, `taskUnsourcedTitle`). Reworded: `reader.distillButtonTitle`, `panels.contextHint`, `works.renameCorpus` — they now say what the control is and does.
- `SPEC.md` §6 — one rule for tooltips.

**Decisions:**
- One shared layer driven by `data-tooltip`, not a wrapper component per control: the diff on each control is one attribute, no extra element disturbs the flex layouts, and the caption is never clipped by a scroll pane (the tray, the document list, the popover's section list) because it renders at the body.
- The concept is called "tooltip" in code and comments (`data-tooltip`, `TooltipLayer`); "caption" stays the glossary term for a distillation quote's caption. Dictionary keys keep the existing `…Title` suffix (from the `title` attribute they replaced) rather than renaming ~40 keys.
- Scope beyond the three named areas: the tray panels, side cards, edit toolbar, and bulk bar traded their native `title` for the same layer, so the reader never sees two tooltip styles in the workspace. Not touched: note cards, the works shelf, settings, the video pane, block marks — their `title` attributes stay native.
- Chinese captions follow the glossary (助手, 笔记, 提炼, 批注, 编辑记录, 选中内容, 项目, 出处, 图谱).
- Verified with Playwright against a seeded local database: 41 checks (text, side, in-viewport, sweep-at-once, Escape/press/scroll hide, keyboard focus, dark theme, zh, touch shows none), zero console errors. Observed and left alone: the fixed Feedback pill covers the rail's More button at 1440×900 (pre-existing).
